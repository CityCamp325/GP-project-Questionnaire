WHY WE MADE THIS PROJECT

Sharing knowledge through learning

During our visit to Zhaoxing, we learned about local medicinal plants and their traditional uses. We wanted to share this knowledge with people who may not have encountered Dong medicine before.

01

What is Dong medicine?

Dong medicine is a form of traditional knowledge connected to Dong communities. It can include knowledge of local plants, preparation methods, traditional uses and ways this knowledge is passed from one generation to another.

02

How are plants traditionally used?

Depending on the plant and traditional practice, plants may be dried, boiled, ground or prepared in other ways. Traditional knowledge can involve knowing which part of a plant is used and how it is traditionally prepared.

03

Why is this knowledge important?

Traditional medicine is also cultural knowledge. Learning about Dong medicine can help us understand local history, culture and the relationship between communities and their natural environment.

Educational note: This website describes traditional uses and cultural knowledge. It is for educational purposes and is not medical advice. Traditional uses should not be assumed to be scientifically proven treatments.
