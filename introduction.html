Zhaoxing

Zhaoxing Dongzhai is located in the southeastern Miao and Dong Autonomous Prefecture of Guizhou Province, China, in the transition zone between the Yungui Plateau and the Xianggui Basin. The main language is still the ethnic language (Dong language, Miao language). Zhaoxing is a hometown of the Dong nationality. There are famous buildings, distinctive traditional culture and rich natural resources.

Dong Medicine

Dong medicine is a national intangible cultural heritage. It is rooted in the deep mountains of southeastern Qianjiang. It has the same origin as traditional Chinese medicine, but the theory, medicine habits and treatment methods have their own characteristics.

Dong doctors positioned people as heaven, earth, qi, water and ren. They believed that the human body relied on "qi" and "water" to maintain balance. Once there was an imbalance, they would get sick, and divided the disease into cold ice and heat.

The medicine pays attention to the six natures and six flavors: hot, cold, collect, disperse, retreat, tonic, sour, astringent, bitter, spicy, fragrant and sweet. Use heat medicine for cold disease, and cold medicine for fever disease. Dong doctors divide the symptoms into 24 major symptoms and 72 minor diseases. They are good at treating rheumatic bone pain, falls and bruises, snake and insect bites. There are traditional techniques of bone straightening, suturing, and herbal external application.

The characteristic therapies of Dong medicine include Dong family medicinal bath, scraping, fresh medicine external application and medicinal wine, etc. Generally, a course of treatment will not be effective at once and make the symptoms disappear immediately, but a long-term process of gradual treatment and conditioning. Dong medicine is not in the form of a pharmacy, but is improvised and dispensed according to the current situation and needs of the village.

WHY WE MADE THIS PROJECT

Sharing knowledge through learning

During our visit to Zhaoxing, we learned about local medicinal plants and their traditional uses. We wanted to share this knowledge with people who may not have encountered Dong medicine before.

01

Who We Are

We are a group of Year 11 students from Shanghai interested in culture, communities, and traditional knowledge. During our visit to Zhaoxing Village, we learned about Dong medicine from a local expert. We created this website to share what we discovered and help others learn about this unique cultural heritage.

02

Why We Chose Dong Medicine in Zhaoxing

We chose Dong medicine because it is closely connected to the culture, environment, and daily life of the Dong people. Our visit to Zhaoxing gave us the opportunity to learn directly from a local expert. We wanted to explore this lesser-known tradition and share its knowledge with a wider audience.

03

Why This Knowledge Is Important

Traditional knowledge reflects how communities have understood and interacted with their environment for generations. Learning about Dong medicine helps us appreciate cultural diversity and understand the importance of preserving local traditions. By sharing what we learned, we hope to raise awareness of Dong medicine and encourage others to explore this cultural heritage.

Educational note: This website describes traditional uses and cultural knowledge. It is for educational purposes and is not medical advice. Traditional uses should not be assumed to be scientifically proven treatments.
